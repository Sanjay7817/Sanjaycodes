Every herb in a DOONGRI™ formulation, with its Sanskrit name, classical Granth reference, traditional role, origin and safety note. Sourcing transparency, every batch.

त्रिफला Triphala

अष्टांग हृदयम् · Ashtanga Hridayam

The classical three-fruit blend (Haritaki, Vibhitaki, Amalaki) supporting digestive regularity and gentle internal cleansing — central to Regulex Churna.

Origin: India · Safety: consult a Vaidya during pregnancy.
❧
हरीतकी Haritaki

चरक संहिता · Charaka Samhita

Traditionally called "the king of medicines" — valued for bowel comfort and healthy transit.

Origin: Sub-Himalayan forests, India.
❧
विभीतकी Vibhitaki

भैषज्य रत्नावली · Bhaishajya Ratnavali

Traditionally used to support digestive balance and overall internal wellness.

Origin: Central India deciduous forests.
❧
आमलकी Amalaki (Amla)

सुश्रुत संहिता · Sushruta Samhita

A cooling, rejuvenating Rasayana fruit, rich in natural vitamin C, with Sheetal Virya (cooling potency).

Origin: India.
❧
नागकेसर Nagkesar

भैषज्य रत्नावली · Bhaishajya Ratnavali

A classical dravya traditionally referenced in Arsha Chikitsa for soothing support.

Origin: Western Ghats, India.
❧
यष्टिमधु Yashtimadhu (Mulethi)

चरक संहिता · Charaka Samhita

A sweet, soothing root traditionally valued for its cooling, comforting properties.

Origin: India & Central Asia · Safety: consult a Vaidya if hypertensive.
❧
दारुहरिद्रा Daruharidra

अष्टांग हृदयम् · Ashtanga Hridayam

A classical herb traditionally used in external lepa preparations for soothing support.

Origin: Himalayan region, India.
❧
कर्पूर Karpura (Camphor)

शीतल वीर्य द्रव्य · Sheetal Virya Dravya

A cooling aromatic traditionally used in external applications such as Sheetlep Ointment.

Origin: India · For external use as directed.
